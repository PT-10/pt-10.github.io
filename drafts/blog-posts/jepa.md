> When a I read something, I often like digging down the rabbit hole to find out where it started. What the "original" was. It helps give me a sense of how and why things are; the way they are today. At the time of writing, it has been around 6 months since Yann LeCun together with Randall Balestriero released a new paper titled "LeJEPA".

# Idea
The core idea of JEPA is to address the following thought - \
How could machines **learn** as **efficiently as humans and animals**? How could machines learn to **reason and plan**? How could machines learn representations of percepts and **action plans at multiple levels of abstraction**, enabling them to reason, predict, and plan **at multiple time horizons**?
